# Ghana Seasonal Farming Calendar


## January
**Weather:** Dry, harmattan winds
**Main Activities:** Land preparation, Bush burning, Cocoa harvesting
**Crops to Plant:** Vegetables (tomato, pepper), Rice (irrigated)
**Farming Advice:** Good time for land clearing and equipment maintenance

## February
**Weather:** Hot and dry
**Main Activities:** Continued land preparation, Seedbed preparation
**Crops to Plant:** Rice (irrigated), Vegetables
**Farming Advice:** Prepare for planting season, check seed quality

## March
**Weather:** Hot, occasional early rains
**Main Activities:** Early planting preparation, Seed procurement
**Crops to Plant:** Early maize, Yam, Vegetables
**Farming Advice:** Time to plant yam and prepare for major season

## April
**Weather:** Rains begin
**Main Activities:** Major season planting begins
**Crops to Plant:** Maize, Rice, Cowpea, Groundnut, Vegetables
**Farming Advice:** Plant immediately after first reliable rains

## May
**Weather:** Heavy rains
**Main Activities:** Peak planting season, Weeding
**Crops to Plant:** Maize, Rice, Cassava, Plantain
**Farming Advice:** Ensure good drainage, watch for pests

## June
**Weather:** Peak rains
**Main Activities:** Weeding, Fertilizer application, Pest control
**Crops to Plant:** Late season maize, Vegetables
**Farming Advice:** Critical time for crop care and pest management

## July
**Weather:** Heavy rains continue
**Main Activities:** Weeding, Disease management
**Crops to Plant:** Minor season preparation
**Farming Advice:** Monitor for fungal diseases, ensure proper drainage

## August
**Weather:** Rains begin to reduce
**Main Activities:** Harvesting early crops, Storage preparation
**Crops to Plant:** Minor season crops
**Farming Advice:** Harvest and store crops properly to avoid post-harvest losses

## September
**Weather:** Moderate rains
**Main Activities:** Minor season planting, Harvesting
**Crops to Plant:** Maize, Cowpea, Vegetables
**Farming Advice:** Good time for second season planting in forest zone

## October
**Weather:** Rains ending
**Main Activities:** Harvesting, Field clearing for next season
**Crops to Plant:** Vegetables, Rice (in some areas)
**Farming Advice:** Focus on harvesting and post-harvest handling

## November
**Weather:** Dry season begins
**Main Activities:** Major harvesting season, Storage
**Crops to Plant:** Dry season vegetables
**Farming Advice:** Ensure proper drying and storage of grains

## December
**Weather:** Cool and dry
**Main Activities:** Storage, Marketing, Land preparation
**Crops to Plant:** Irrigated crops, Vegetables
**Farming Advice:** Good time for equipment maintenance and planning
