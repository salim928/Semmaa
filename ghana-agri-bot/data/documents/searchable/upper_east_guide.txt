
# Upper East Region Farming Guide

## Climate and Weather
- Climate Type: Sudan savanna
- Annual Rainfall: 950-1150mm annually

## Major Crops
Millet, Sorghum, Rice, Groundnut, Cowpea

## Soil Types  
Savanna Ochrosols, Lithosols

## Challenges and Solutions
Erratic rainfall, Soil fertility decline, Land degradation
