
# Northern Region Farming Guide

## Climate and Weather
- Climate Type: Sudan savanna
- Annual Rainfall: 750-1100mm annually

## Major Crops
Maize, Millet, Sorghum, Yam, Rice, Cowpea

## Soil Types  
Savanna Ochrosols, Groundwater laterite

## Challenges and Solutions
Drought, Soil erosion, Short growing season
